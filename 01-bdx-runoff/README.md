# BDX Runoff Business Analysis Project

## Overview

This project explores the business analysis, data requirements, process controls, and SQL opportunities involved in a BDX runoff settlement and export process.

The work is based on a Business Requirements Document (BRD) covering settlement processing, payment allocations, Paid BDX generation, export controls, reconciliation, and exception handling.

## Project Objectives

- Translate business requirements into structured data requirements.
- Understand the relationships between business processes and database design.
- Identify data quality checks and reconciliation controls.
- Develop SQL examples to support validation and reporting.
- Demonstrate how a Business Analyst connects business needs with technical solutions.

## Planned Project Contents

- Business process overview
- Logical data model
- SQL proof of concept
- Data validation and reconciliation queries
- Test scenarios and expected results

## Status

**In progress** — project structure and supporting materials are being developed.

## Note on Data

Any published examples will use synthetic or appropriately anonymised data. Confidential business information will not be included.
