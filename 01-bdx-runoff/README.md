# BDX Runoff — Business Analysis & Data Design

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how business analysis can translate a complex insurance settlement and reporting process into structured business requirements, data requirements, process controls, and opportunities for SQL-based validation.

The project is based on a BDX runoff settlement and export scenario. It explores how settlement information moves through a process, how data relationships support reporting, and how reconciliation and exception handling can improve confidence in the resulting outputs.

## Business Problem

A controlled settlement and reporting process needs to ensure that:

- Settlement information is captured and processed consistently.
- Payment allocations can be traced and validated.
- Reporting outputs are generated accurately.
- Data can be reconciled between processing stages.
- Exceptions are identifiable and auditable.

## Business Analysis Approach

The project focuses on the following areas:

- **Requirements analysis** — translating business needs into clear, testable requirements.
- **Process analysis** — understanding the movement of information through the settlement and export lifecycle.
- **Data analysis and design** — identifying key data entities, relationships, and required fields.
- **Business controls** — defining validation, reconciliation, and exception-handling requirements.
- **Testing** — developing scenarios to check expected behaviour, edge cases, and data integrity.
- **SQL proof of concept** — exploring how queries can support validation, reconciliation, and reporting.

## Planned Deliverables

- Sanitised business requirements overview
- High-level process flow
- Logical data model
- SQL validation and reconciliation examples
