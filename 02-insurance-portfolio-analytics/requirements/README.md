# Business Requirements

## Purpose

Document the business questions, reporting outputs, financial measures, assumptions and acceptance criteria for the Insurance Portfolio Analytics project.

## Planned contents

- Business reporting requirements
- Portfolio, group, department and business-level reporting
- Product channels and insurance markets
- Premium, claims and loss-ratio definitions
- Reporting-period rules
- Business assumptions and outstanding decisions
- Requirements traceability and acceptance criteria

## Status

Initial project setup. Detailed requirements will be developed before the reporting data model is finalised.
